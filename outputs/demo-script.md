# LexCatalyst demo script (about 3 minutes)

## [0:00] Landing page and sign-in
**Click:** **Sign in with Google**.

> "Thank you, Lauren. This is the LexCatalyst landing page, and we'll sign in with our email account. LexCatalyst is a legal workspace for Singapore firms. Today we'll follow one case from start to finish: the Tan family's purchase of a Bishan condominium. Jane is the associate on it, and Sarah, a senior associate, supervises her."

## [0:20] Jane asks Birdie what to prioritise
**Click:** Switch to **Jane** → **Workboard** → **Birdie** → **Ask** → type:
`What should I prioritise today across all my matters?`

> "Jane starts her day by asking Birdie, her AI mentor, what to work on. Birdie reads her live Workboard. It sees that the CPF and stamp-duty questions are overdue, and that the Bishan option-to-purchase review is due today."

## [0:40] Jane opens the Bishan matter
**Click:** The card **"Review option to purchase for Bishan condominium"** → the matter link.

> "Jane picks the Bishan purchase. Everything for the case is in one place: the review notes, the tasks and the LexChats."

## [0:55] Birdie knows the matter
**Click:** **Birdie** → type:
`Where are we on this matter?`

> "Birdie knows which matter Jane has open. It tells her that Sarah sent her first note back with comments, and that her revised note is waiting for Sarah's review."

## [1:15] Sarah's comments on the first draft
**Click:** The OTP task → **Round** picker → **1 · Returned** (**Bishan OTP review note v1.pdf**) → click each of the four highlighted passages.

| # | Jane wrote | Sarah's redline | Sarah's reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ~~the option must be exercised within 14 days~~ | the option must be exercised by 4pm on 20 October 2026 | Give the exact deadline and time. A period on its own invites argument about when it starts. |
| 2 | ~~ABSD will be payable at 20% on this purchase~~ | ABSD may apply. Before advising, we will confirm the purchasers' citizenship and existing property holdings, and check the current IRAS rates | Never state a stamp-duty rate or eligibility from an assumption. Confirm the facts and the current rates first. |
| 3 | ~~hand over the unit in good condition~~ | deliver vacant possession | 'Good condition' is not the contractual standard. Use vacant possession and say what it covers. |
| 4 | ~~We will send the client our advice once the seller's solicitors reply.~~ | I will flag open points to Sarah for review before anything is sent to the client. | Nothing goes to the client before the supervising lawyer has reviewed it. |

> "Sarah redlines Jane's note directly on the document, and every change comes with a reason. Give the exact option deadline instead of '14 days'. Never state a stamp-duty rate based on an assumption. Say 'vacant possession', not 'good condition'. And nothing goes to the client until Sarah has reviewed it."

## [1:40] Birdie turns the comments into lessons
**Click:** **Birdie** → **Review** tab. Then **Ask** → type:
`Why did Sarah change my stamp duty paragraph?`

> "Birdie turns Sarah's comments into four lessons that Jane keeps. Jane can also ask why a change was made. Birdie explains that she needs to confirm the clients' citizenship and existing property holdings, and check the current IRAS rates, before advising on ABSD."

## [2:05] The revised draft
**Click:** **Round** picker → **2 (latest) · Ready for review** (**Bishan OTP review note v2.pdf**).

> "Jane's second draft applies every lesson, and it's now waiting for Sarah."

## [2:15] Chrome extension
**Click:** Gmail draft → highlight the text → **Birdie toolbar icon** → type:
`Check this email for the Bishan purchase before I send it.`

Draft text to paste into Gmail beforehand:
> *Dear Sirs, we confirm our clients will exercise the option within 14 days. Kindly confirm the seller will hand over the unit in good condition on completion.*

> "Jane doesn't only work in our app, so Birdie goes with her. Here she's drafting an email to the seller's solicitors. Birdie catches the mistakes Sarah corrected last time: '14 days' needs an exact date, and 'good condition' should say 'vacant possession'. It also reminds Jane to have Sarah review the email before she sends it."

## [2:45] Close
> "Birdie helped Jane decide what to work on, explained Sarah's comments, and applied them to her next draft and to her email. Sarah reviews once, and Jane gets better with every round. Thank you."
